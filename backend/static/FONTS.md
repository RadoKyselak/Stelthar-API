# Fonts

`instrument-sans.woff2` and `instrument-serif.woff2` are the Latin subsets of
Instrument Sans and Instrument Serif as served by Google Fonts
(fonts.gstatic.com). They are self-hosted, so visitors' browsers contact no
font service.

Both typefaces are published by Instrument
(https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans and
https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif) under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). Each file carries its
copyright notice and license in its own metadata.
