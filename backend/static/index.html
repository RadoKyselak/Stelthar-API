Is that number right?

Check a claim against the government's own published figures, with a citation to the exact table and row.

How it works

Mirador reads the figure, period and place in a claim, pulls that exact number from the agency’s own data, and compares them at the precision the claim was written to. Close calls are Mostly accurate. Every answer shows the published row it came from. When no official series covers a claim, it searches published sources and only reports what a source backs.

What can I check?

Mirador is independent. It is not a government website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency. Figures come from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Bureau of Economic Analysis, the Census Bureau and the Treasury.
