Add to Chrome

optional: for a claim that says “is” or names no date

Paste a sentence, a whole article or a link to one. Every claim in it is checked against the agency’s own data.

Try one

    1. 1

      Paste or highlight

      A sentence, a whole article, or the link to a page. In Chrome, highlight text, right-click and choose Check with Project Mirador.

    2. 2

      Read from the agency

      Each figure comes from the data service of the agency that publishes it: the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Census Bureau, the Treasury and ten more.

    3. 3

      See the receipt

      The official figure, the rows it was read from, the series it belongs to and a citation you can copy. How a claim is judged.
