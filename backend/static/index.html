Mirador Chrome extension

Is that number right?

Check any claim against official U.S. government data.

How it works

Mirador reads the figure, period and place in a claim, pulls that exact number from the agency’s own data, and compares them at the precision the claim was written to. Close calls are Mostly accurate. Every answer cites its source. When no official series covers a claim, it searches published sources and only reports what a source backs.

What can I check?

Data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, Bureau of Economic Analysis, Census Bureau and Treasury. Independent; not affiliated with any agency. API
