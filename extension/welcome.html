Mirador

Check any number against the official record.

Three steps, under a minute.

  1. Pin Mirador

    Click the puzzle piece in Chrome’s toolbar, then the pin next to Mirador.

  2. Check your first claim

    Highlight the sentence below, right-click it and choose Fact-check with Mirador.

    The U.S. unemployment rate averaged 3.6% in 2023.

  3. Use it anywhere

    • On any page: highlight a sentence or a whole article, right-click, Fact-check with Mirador.
    • Click the Mirador icon, or press Ctrl+Shift+Y, and type a claim.
    • In the address bar: type mir, press Tab, then a measure like “unemployment 2023”.
